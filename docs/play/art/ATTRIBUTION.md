# In-game artwork credits

These compact runtime images use the exact poses, RGB565 colors, transparency, and timing from the game’s installed 32px artwork. Atlas frames are deduplicated idle poses. No original source sheet or full source pack is included.

Digimon character designs and official game artwork remain with their respective rights holders. This fan demo does not grant or transfer artwork rights. These third-party assets receive no project-wide or open-content license from this demo.
