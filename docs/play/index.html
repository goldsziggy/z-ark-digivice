PLAY ON THE ROUND SCREEN

Your finger is the controller.

On a phone, use taps and swipes. With a mouse, click and drag on the same screen.

Browse eggs, Home panels, companions and evolution paths with a left/right swipe or edge tap. Use the labeled screen buttons to choose and go back.

In Manual battles, choose a move left/right, then swipe up to use it. During capture, a fresh tap in the main area commits the timing immediately.

Touch behavior follows installed firmware 171cda7. These are adapted browser screens. Physical walking, nearby radio play and hardware settings remain device-only.
