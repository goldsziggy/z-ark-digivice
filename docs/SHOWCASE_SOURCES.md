# Showcase sources and notices

This independent fan-made z-ark Digivice showcase pairs real device recordings, native simulator samples and actual enclosure geometry.

- **Hardware footage:** two owner-supplied recordings, presented as four continuous, silent close-ups at original speed. Public copies frame the display and bezel; surrounding pixels are masked. Anonymous fingertips remain where they overlap the touch display. The source camera files, original audio, upload identifiers and personal surroundings are not published. The recorded capture is automatic; the manual-flick sequence is simulated.
- **Simulator:** native C++ UI rendered from the immutable sample firmware baseline `f74ee4c132b4b9bc6407d99bbc20b13af9dfd962`, with prepared states and verified local SD assets. These samples are not labeled as the latest firmware. Walking inputs and nearby peers are synthetic; no physical radio exchange or live device timing is established by them. No personal save was used.
- **Enclosure:** exact frozen C14-P19 touch-only printed-part meshes. Official Waveshare 1.46-inch board/display reference geometry and a 36 × 67 × 10 mm battery design envelope provide context. The display is intentionally blank in CAD views. Optional NFC parts and nominal fastener profiles are labeled. Exploded spacing is illustrative; physical fit remains unverified.
